Data Deletion Request

At RessDe, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data.

If you would like to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data (including your name, email, phone number, address, and order history), please send an email to:

ressde.support@gmail.com

Please use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request" and include the email address or phone number associated with your RessDe account.

We will process your request and delete your data from our servers within 7 business days, except for any data we are legally required to retain (such as tax records).
